# Guidance for the six speech parts

Research checked 2026-10-14. The writing guidance is practical paraphrase, not quotation. Variation drills are original exercises. The primary basis is Quintilian's *Institutio Oratoria*, H. E. Butler's public-domain translation (1920), hosted by LacusCurtius. Its courtroom setting is adapted to modern speeches; its advice does not make every part mandatory for every occasion.

| Part | Useful qualities | Primary reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | Prepare a receptive, attentive audience; connect the opening to this particular case; avoid ostentation. | [IV.1.5–10, 33–34, 60–62](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Quintilian/Institutio_Oratoria/4A*.html) |
| Background | Explain people, events and causes clearly; remove irrelevant detail without sacrificing comprehension; make surprising facts understandable. | [IV.2.31–45, 52–53](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Quintilian/Institutio_Oratoria/4B*.html) |
| Position and roadmap | Distinguish agreed from disputed matters; use clear, concise divisions without redundant subdivisions; deliver the announced order. | [IV.5.1–3, 22–28](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Quintilian/Institutio_Oratoria/4C*.html) |
| Supporting case | Explain the reasoning; establish doubtful premises; distinguish individually strong arguments from cumulative evidence. | [V.12.4–8](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Quintilian/Institutio_Oratoria/5C*.html), [V.14.1–8, 13–17](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Quintilian/Institutio_Oratoria/5D*.html) |

## Interpretation notes

- IV.5.3 explicitly rejects an arbitrary limit of three propositions. IV.5.22 says partition is not always necessary or useful. This supports flexible practice rather than a universal formula.
- V.14.13–17 is the most precise support for establishing uncertain premises; the earlier discussion at V.14.1–8 explains argument components.
- V.12.4–5 permits weaker evidence to gain strength cumulatively. The app should not imply that every individually inconclusive item must be discarded.
- V.13.42–44 also supports practicing an intelligent opponent's case rather than manufacturing easy objections. The current drill is an original application of that principle.
- These sources explain how the sections function. They are not evidence that composing all six on every occasion is necessary, or that drills guarantee expertise.

No modern source was needed for this small addition. No direct quotations are used in the app guidance.
