- [Ronald Reagan, remarks at the Brandenburg Gate, June 12, 1987 — Reagan Presidential Library](https://www.reaganlibrary.gov/archives/speech/remarks-east-west-relations-brandenburg-gate-west-berlin). Uses all speech paragraphs, from “Thank you very much” through the final farewell, including the closing response to demonstrators. Omits the archivist’s subsequent NOTE and removes three printed page markers. Keeps bracketed translations and the laughter marker, plus the source’s punctuation and apparent transcription quirks. These are official presidential addresses produced in federal duties, included as public-domain government speech texts. Website navigation, modern introductory commentary, and images are not reproduced.

The study boundaries, headings, explanations, device identifications, and practice exercises are editorial readings, not claims by the archives or original section divisions. Neither speech is forced into exactly six sections: Kennedy’s pledges and proposals recur, and Reagan returns to rebuttal after his main conclusion. Historical political claims remain the speakers’ claims, not independent findings of this app.

Validation: assembled section text compared with every source speech paragraph, in order, after the explicitly documented removal of page markers. All eight selected device quotations checked as exact substrings of their own section. Device examples are selective rather than an exhaustive inventory. No UI changes or browser checks were made in this content-only task.
